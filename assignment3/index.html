Part 5 - Non-linear Components

ISOMAP with 3 nearest neighbours is compared with PCA as a nonlinear dimensionality-reduction method.

Both PCA and ISOMAP separate Setosa clearly. Versicolor and Virginica still have some overlap. For this dataset, PCA is preferable because it gives a clear separation while also providing explained-variance information. ISOMAP is more useful when the data lies on a strongly curved or nonlinear manifold.
